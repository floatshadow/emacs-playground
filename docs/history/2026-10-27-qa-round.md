## Open decisions

- **Watch it latch.** After Watch it, a learner who completes the step with their own keys gets no reaction until they
  press Do it yourself, also when their first key stopped the demo (api.md §4.1, the engine's latch). A UX decision.
- **Habit cards.** They are suppressed only for the current lesson's and step's text, so "C-n is not new file" fires in
  0.1 step 2 and in 1.2. There is no card for VS Code's Ctrl+G (`C-g 35` inserts "35"); habits match single keys only.
- **Fast-forwarded steps** show as "Later" above the current step, and Continue to an all-watched lesson stays on its
  last step. Both are as documented.
- **Start over wording.** It says files and Emacs stay as they are, but an open lesson tab reloads with `setup.reset`
  and discards unsaved edits (frontend.md §5).
- **Workspace is the checkpoint repository** (backend.md §3.9): project.el sees `/work` as a project ("Project: work",
  `emacs.d` in `project-files`). Hiding it needs a separate `GIT_DIR`.
- **Skip this step** is reachable only after an I'm stuck report, so it disappears with `features.stuckReports: false`.
- **Preview CSP** `sandbox allow-scripts allow-same-origin` lets a learner's HTML, opened directly, make credentialed
  same-origin requests to `/api` and `/s`. Needs a separate origin or dropping `allow-same-origin`.
- **Repository hygiene.** No LICENSE file; `deploy/config.json` is tracked although `make local` writes it; unused
  devDependencies `@playwright/test` and `json-schema-to-typescript`.
- **Tooling.** `make dev` and `playctl dev` do the same job with different strictness and signal handling; `rpcKeys`
  maps supervisor errors to 400/409 by message text instead of sentinel errors.
- **Content and roadmap.** No zh-CN curriculum content and 67 of 333 UI strings translated (zh-CN hidden until
  complete); `.eld` window-state layouts dropped by the scenario loader; planned TODOs (image major-version check,
  per-learner remote box, registries egress proxy, `DELETE /me`, export-user, latency histograms, govulncheck, Renovate).
- **Known limitations** (docs/notes.md): reset-step does not turn off minor modes a demo turned on; the 3.5
  display-buffer rule after Go to this step or Watch it; lesson 3.1's `close` demo is relative to the selected window;
  plain Ctrl+/ reaches Emacs as `C-_` through tmux.
