Part 15 is practice: each lesson says what to make, not which keys to press, and checks only the result. Use any keys you know; Watch it shows one way, and Skip is there if you get stuck.

:::vscode
The same exercise as writing a README in VS Code with word wrap at 80 columns, except that here the line breaks are real: a paragraph is filled, not wrapped by the window.
:::

## The brief

`notes/essay.md` is an outline: three headings, with a TODO line under each.

- Under the first heading, write at least 80 words: what you keep in plain text, and why.
- Under the other two, a few sentences each.
- Keep the three headings exactly as they are.
- No line longer than 80 characters. Type freely and fill each paragraph afterwards: :try[M-q] fills the one the cursor is in.

Save as you go: the checks read the file on disk.

::steps
