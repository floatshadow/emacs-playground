#+title: My Org settings
#+author: me

A literate configuration: the prose says why, the blocks are the code.
C-c C-v t writes every block, in order, to my-org.el.

* Header
The first line of the file, and the name other files can require.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
;;; my-org.el --- my Org settings  -*- lexical-binding: t; -*-
(provide 'my-org)
#+end_src

* Tags
Keep tags right after the heading text instead of in a far column.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(setq org-tags-column 0)
#+end_src
